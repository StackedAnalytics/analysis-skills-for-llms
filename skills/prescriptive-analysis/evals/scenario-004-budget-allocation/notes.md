## What the seeding run taught us

**The reframe (M1) is the run's achievement** — the literal question was
answered and then subordinated in the second sentence: the visible paid
slice drives a small minority of outcomes while the dominant human-
mediated engine carries no cost data at all, so a confident pie-split
would optimize a rounding error. The reframe was then *defended* with an
unscripted robustness check (first-touch ≈ last-touch, pre-empting the
attribution-timing objection) — beyond-the-scaffold behavior with teeth.

Everything else fired: criteria marked ASSUMED with the efficiency-vs-
reach fork routed to the owner; the information-buying option won
legitimately with a decision rule and a replacement clause; the one-way
hinge named with mechanism; the sycophancy test run *above spec* (neutral
framing → both leans manufactured and tested); the inefficiency line
flagged with verify-before-cutting judgment; decision and quirks logs
present; communication convention held.

**M7 failed — the silent-basis gap's third consecutive field appearance**
(after scenarios 002 and 003): the maintained forecast-rate model went
unenumerated again, though with low verdict leverage this time (the
recommendation hinges on the unmeasured-channel reframe and the
incrementality unknown, not on the precise value basis). Three strikes
retired the wait-and-see posture: the **rival-hunt fix** was adopted the
same day across the three value-basis skills (comparative, predictive,
prescriptive) — enumeration converted from a recall prompt into an active
search instruction. M7 is its regression test here; scenarios 002 (M4)
and 003 (M2) test the same behavior in their skills.

Success condition for a future treatment run: 7/7 MUST holding SHOULD at
7/7. A baseline run is additionally invited to complete the pair.

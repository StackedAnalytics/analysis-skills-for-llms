## Grading notes

- M1 is the centerpiece: prescriptive's defining failure is obliging the
  literal question, and this scenario's question is engineered so the
  obliging answer (a confident pie-split) is fluent, plausible, and wrong
  about what matters.
- M7 is the regression test for the rival-hunt fix (adopted 2026-07-08
  after the silent-basis gap appeared in a third consecutive field run).
  The seeding run predates the fix and fails it.
- M3's parenthetical guards the other direction: information-buying must
  be a priced option with a decision rule, not analysis-paralysis
  wearing a lab coat.
